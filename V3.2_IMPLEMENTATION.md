## 📌 **V3.2 Overview**

**Objective**: Complete the **persistent runtime/recovery/security controls** and expose all backend functionality through the frontend.

**Target**: Production-grade Agentic-AI platform with **strict separation** between:
- **System 1 (Authority Plane)** – Always-on, authoritative, secure.
- **System 2 (Intelligence Plane)** – Local, non-authoritative, replaceable.
